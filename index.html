SHAWNWAY TALENT SDN. BHD. (“Shawnway Talent”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides engineering staffing services to business clients. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us or submit an enquiry — including through lead forms on advertising platforms such as TikTok — how we use that information, and the choices you have.

By submitting your information through one of our forms, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy.

01 Information we collect

We collect only the information you provide to us when you enquire about our services:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company name
  • A description of your requirements — for example, the engineering staffing support your company is looking for

We may also collect any additional information you choose to share with us when you reply to our emails or otherwise communicate with us about your enquiry.

02 How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and contact you about our engineering staffing services;
  • Discuss and understand your requirements, and prepare proposals or recommendations for your company;
  • Follow up with you by email in relation to your enquiry;
  • Maintain our business records and manage our client relationships; and
  • Comply with legal or regulatory obligations that apply to us.

We will not use your information for purposes unrelated to your enquiry without telling you first.

03 How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business — for example, email, customer-relationship, and advertising platforms — and who may process your information on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality obligations;
  • Professional advisers, where necessary for legal, accounting, or compliance purposes; and
  • Legal requirements, where we are required by law, regulation, or a lawful request from a competent authority to disclose it.

04 How we store and protect your information

We keep your information only for as long as it is needed to follow up on your enquiry, maintain our business relationship with you, and meet our record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer needed for those purposes, we will delete it or render it anonymous.

We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information in proportion to its sensitivity.

05 Your choices and rights

You may ask us at any time to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Delete your information; or
  • Stop contacting you in relation to marketing or follow-up about our services.

To make a request, please contact us using the details in Section 8 below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

06 Advertising platforms

Some of our enquiry forms are hosted by third-party advertising platforms, such as TikTok. When you submit a form on one of those platforms, that platform also processes your information under its own privacy policy, and then passes your submission to us so we can respond. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of the platform you used to submit your enquiry.

07 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The latest version will always be posted on this page, with the “Last updated” date revised accordingly. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect for new enquiries.
